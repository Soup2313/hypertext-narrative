A grizzly encounter

    Instinctively you turn around and start running back to your car. You do not even give a glance at what you left behind. Fear has taken over your mind.

    What was that? Who was that? You have to get out of here! While your mind is occupied with questions you fail to notice the bear. Your panic has spooked and unleashed the bear’s predatory instincts. You are mauled to death.

    You were not brave and die.
